Research portfolio

Hello, I’m Minseo Kwak.

I study lipid-based delivery of mRNA and siRNA for cancer therapy.

My research experience spans targeted liposomal siRNA delivery for cancer therapy, pulmonary mRNA delivery, and the in vitro and in vivo evaluation of nanoparticle-based platforms.

I am interested in integrating formulation design with biological context to develop potent RNA carriers tailored to the cargo, target tissue, and route of administration.
